# Supply Chain Visibility & Optimization – Milestone 4

## Project Summary
This milestone extends the previous Power BI report by adding a **Warehouse Efficiency Dashboard** and an **Executive Dashboard**. The dashboards provide insights into warehouse performance, inventory management, supplier reliability, and overall supply chain operations using interactive visualizations and DAX measures.

## Warehouse Utilization Methodology
Warehouse utilization is calculated to determine how effectively warehouse capacity is being used. It helps identify underutilized and highly occupied warehouses for better inventory planning.

## Throughput & Productivity KPIs
- **Warehouse Throughput:** Measures the total stock/orders processed by each warehouse.
- **Warehouse Productivity:** Evaluates warehouse performance based on units shipped and average units processed per order.
- **Utilization KPIs:** Maximum, Minimum, and Average Utilization percentages are used to compare warehouse efficiency.

## Executive Dashboard Methodology
The Executive Dashboard combines important business KPIs from all milestones into a single view. It includes sales trends, fulfillment rate, supplier reliability, inventory turnover, warehouse utilization, dead stock, and order statistics with interactive filters for detailed analysis.

## Forecasting
Forecasting is implemented on the sales trend chart using Power BI Analytics to estimate future sales based on historical data.

## Performance Optimization
- Created reusable DAX measures.
- Optimized the data model by removing unnecessary fields.
- Applied slicers and drill-through for better navigation.
- Used suitable visuals to improve report readability and performance.

## Business Insights
- Warehouse utilization differs across warehouses, indicating opportunities for better capacity management.
- High supplier reliability contributes to strong fulfillment performance.
- Monitoring inventory turnover and dead stock helps improve inventory efficiency.
- Sales trend forecasting supports future planning and decision-making.
